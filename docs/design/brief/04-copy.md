# 4. Words

Words are the product here: the manual has none, and we add them. Use this real copy in the designs. Nothing should be placeholder text.

## Voice

- **Plain verbs, sentence case, no filler.** "Tap 2 dowels into the long panel." Never "Simply proceed to insert the dowels."
- **Say what a control does.** "Next step", "Replay", "Choose a file". Never "Submit" or "Continue" on its own, except the one Continue that follows a card telling you to go and do something.
- **One name per thing, all the way through.** It is always a "manual", a "step", a "drawing" (the printed picture) and "the 3D view". It is never "document", "stage", "diagram" in one place and "image" in another.
- **Problems say what happened and what to do next.** No apology, no "Oops", no "Something went wrong" when we know what went wrong.
- **Honest about the AI.** When it was unsure, say so in plain words. When a warning is our inference, call it "Possible mistake". Never "common mistake": we have no evidence it is common.
- **Read aloud test.** Every instruction should sound right said to someone across a room.

## Product line

> **EzAssemble**
> The manual shows you what. We show you how.

A plainer alternative, if the line above feels like a slogan: "Assembly manuals, explained one step at a time."
